## Natural Earth land / world-atlas 2.0.2

`land.js` contains the GeoJSON conversion of https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/world-atlas@2.0.2/land-110m.json. Arc quantization is retained and coordinates are rounded to five decimal places. Source: https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas. Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
